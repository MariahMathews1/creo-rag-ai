# Translation data governance

Translation pairs are local controlled evidence. `ai_processing_allowed` defaults to false. Phase 10 can transmit only minimized excerpts from verified-successful, explicitly consented records through the centralized policy layer and only after human invocation. Public-web retrieval, fine-tuning, manuals, unrelated programs, and automatic cross-machine widening remain disabled. Audit events contain IDs, status changes, hashes/counts, safe provider metadata, and review metadata—not credentials, hidden reasoning, or full prompts.

Only verified-successful records are future-retrieval eligible by default. Deprecated and invalid records are excluded from the normal library and explorer. Reference programs are linked only by an explicit optional relationship; approved G-code without its corresponding CL is not a translation example.

Manual documentation, organizational-standard evidence, and historical translation evidence remain distinct authority types. G-POST reads historical counts and observations but never mutates mappings from them. Production controls such as authentication, authorization, malware scanning, formal signatures, encryption policy, retention, and qualified simulation remain outside this proof of concept.
